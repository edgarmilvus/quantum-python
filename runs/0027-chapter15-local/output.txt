--- 02_kernel

three layers reach 1.000 and four fall back to 0.833: a sharper kernel is not

--- 03_shots

so the kernel is robust to sampling, and the next demo shows what it is not
robust to: a phase error that moves one pair from 0.49 to 0.00, which no
penalty can undo because it is a bias rather than a fluctuation.
--- 04_device
the device's kernel still beats the classical one at a small penalty, 0.833
against 0.667, and the exact kernel is perfect at every penalty. The gap the
machine opened is the difference between a 1.000 and a 0.833, and it is made
of one collapsed pair and a diagonal of 0.983 instead of 1.000.
--- 05_cost

this is why the honest claim for quantum kernels is not speed:
it is that the kernel is a different, possibly better, geometry, and that
the price of measuring it is quadratic in the points before it buys anything.
